About Us

Caring for the Girl Child.

Namuleri International Girls Academy (NIGA) was born out of the legacy of "Mai Namuleri" (Caring Mother), whose perseverance in hardship inspired the vision to provide quality education to talented girls from underprivileged backgrounds.

We select high-performing girls (As, Bs, Cs in national exams) who lack financial means and nurture them into ethical, confident global leaders equipped with ICT, critical thinking, and spiritual guidance.

Photo: Namuleri Girls in School Uniform

Our Mission

Empowering girls through quality education to become confident leaders, innovators, and responsible global citizens who create positive impact.

Academics

A Future-Focused Curriculum

We deliver the Malawi National Secondary Curriculum alongside intensive ICT training, entrepreneurship skills, and international languages.

Junior Secondary (Forms 1–2)

Foundational education in sciences, humanities, mathematics, ICT, and languages.

Senior Secondary (Forms 3–4)

MSCE preparation focusing on distinctions, university admission readiness, and leadership projects.

Language Department

Support a Girl Child

Sponsor an Education, Change a Life.

Over 90% of our students come from underprivileged backgrounds. Parents contribute 20kg of beans, while the rest is funded through Namuleri farms and well-wishers.

With $300/month (or custom donations), you can sponsor a girl's tuition, standardized meals, textbooks, uniform, and medical care. We encourage sponsors to build a personal bond with the student they support.

Admissions

Selection & Enrolment

Admission is open to girls aged 12 to 16 regardless of place of origin, selected based on academic performance and financial need.

  1. Application & Selection.
    Candidates selected based on strong Primary School Leaving Certificate (PSLC) results.
  2. Verification.
    Assessment of household income level.
  3. Parental Contribution.
    Parent/guardian contribution of 20kg beans per term.
  4. Admission & Orientation.
    Full scholarship covering tuition, meals, and board.
